## Reservation Policy

- Reservations accepted up to 30 days in advance
- Walk-ins welcome but recommended to book on weekends
- Cancellation must be made 24 hours before — late cancellation fee: $15pp for groups of 4+
- Maximum party size for online booking: 10 people (larger groups call directly)
- Outdoor terrace seating: available Apr–Oct, first-come first-served
- Waiting list available via website during peak times

## Dietary & Allergen Information

- **Vegetarian** items marked V on menu
- **Vegan** options: Rigatoni alla Norma (request vegan cheese), Margherita pizza (no cheese), mixed salad, most starters
- **Gluten-free pasta** available on request (+$2.50) — not suitable for coeliac (shared kitchen)
- **Nut allergy:** kitchen uses pine nuts and pistachios — please inform staff
- **Dairy-free:** possible for most pasta dishes — ask your server
- Full allergen matrix available on website under "Menu → Allergens"

## FAQ

**Can I bring my own cake for a birthday?**  
Yes, corkage fee of $5 applies. Please inform us when booking.

**Do you take walk-ins?**  
Yes, especially weekday lunches. Weekends we recommend booking — waiting list available.

**Is there a kids menu?**  
Yes: mini pasta, pizza Margherita, chicken strips — all $11. Includes a soft drink and gelato.

**Do you host private events?**  
Yes, the private dining room seats up to 20 guests. Email events@bellacucina.com for packages.

**Can I order ahead for a specific time?**  
Yes, scheduled ordering available on the website up to 2 hours in advance.

**Is the wine list available online?**  
Full wine list at bellacucina.com/wine — sommelier recommendations updated monthly.
